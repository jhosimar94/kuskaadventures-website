Lilah and Melody: Kuska Adventures — Episode Guide

Ten episodes, each about one big feeling, with a little magic and a Quechua word. Each episode comes in two short parts, and the full episode follows. New every week on YouTube.

After watching, ask: What were you feeling? What were you doing? Why?

Episode 1 A Place for Us

Lilah and Melody can't wait for their family magic. When Papa is too busy to look at their castle plan, the sisters end up far away on a mountain, where they meet Mumo, a gentle giant who has been called "too little."

Big feeling: Wanting to be heard · Quechua: Kuska (together)

▶ Watch Part 1 · Part 2 comes out Friday, October 23

Episode 2 Which One Is Mine?

The sisters test for magic at sunrise. Nothing works... until Miski runs off with Grandma's headband.

Big feeling: Impatience · Quechua: Inti (sun)

Episode 3 The First Morning

Melody's first day at the learning courtyard. When a shy girl needs help, Melody forgets her nerves.

Big feeling: Feeling nervous and left out · Quechua: a greeting word

Episode 4 Just One More Jump

Too much magic at once turns the obstacle course into a big jumble. Grandpa Rumi helps, and the girls learn: stop, breathe, one small thing.

Big feeling: Too excited to wait · Quechua: a patience word

Episode 5 Mumo's Surprise

Mumo comes back! But the whole village is staring, and Mumo is nervous. The girls plan a welcome.

Big feeling: Making someone feel at home · Quechua: Kuska (together)

Episode 6 No Means No

Cousin Sumaq is making a private surprise. The sisters peek anyway, and learn that magic doesn't change what someone said.

Big feeling: Respecting a no · Quechua: asking and declining

Episode 7 Fast Isn't First

Cousin Wayra discovers super speed, and Lilah feels a little smaller. A mixed-up message shows them how to be a team.

Big feeling: Jealousy · Quechua: Mayu (river)

Episode 8 Small Brave Steps

The gliding lane brings back Lilah's worry about her arm. Melody learns to ask what kind of help her sister wants.

Big feeling: Fear after getting hurt · Quechua: a comfort word

Episode 9 Everybody's Turn

All the cousins want something different for the play castle. Then Mama shows the magic she's been keeping secret.

Big feeling: Making room for others' ideas · Quechua: Kuska (together)

Episode 10 A Place for All of Us

Opening day at Kuska Castle! Everything goes wrong, until the whole family works together. And a giant friend arrives.

Big feeling: Shared pride · Quechua: Tupananchiskama (until we meet again)
