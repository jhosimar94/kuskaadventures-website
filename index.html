About the show

A family cartoon from the Andes of Peru

Lilah and Melody: Kuska Adventures is an animated series for kids ages 3 to 7 and the grown-ups who watch with them. Sisters Lilah (7) and Melody (6) live in Nayaluma, a magical village in the Sacred Valley of Peru. Their family magic arrives slowly, through funny mistakes and brave choices, and every episode is about one big feeling: being heard, saying sorry, waiting, being brave, including others. Each episode also teaches a word in Quechua, the living language of the Andes. New episodes every week on YouTube, in English and Spanish (Lilah y Melody: Aventuras Kuska). See the episode guide.
